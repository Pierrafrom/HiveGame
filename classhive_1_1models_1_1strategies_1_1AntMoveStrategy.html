Detailed Description

Movement strategy for the Ant piece in the Hive game.

This class implements the specific movement rules for the Ant piece, allowing it to traverse multiple spaces on the board, provided it stays adjacent to other pieces.

Calculates the possible moves for the Ant piece.

Parameters

playerThe player that owns the Ant.
Returns
A vector of Hex objects representing all reachable positions.

This method overrides getPossibleMoves to provide the unique movement behavior for the Ant. The Ant can move to any location on the perimeter of connected pieces, following the sliding rule that keeps it adjacent to other pieces.
