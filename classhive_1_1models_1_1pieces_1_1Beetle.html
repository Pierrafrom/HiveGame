Detailed Description

Represents a Beetle piece in the Hive game.

The Beetle class inherits from the abstract Piece class and encapsulates the unique characteristics and movement behavior of the Beetle piece in Hive. The Beetle can climb on top of other pieces, affecting the hive structure.
