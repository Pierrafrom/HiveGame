Detailed Description

Represents a Grasshopper piece in the Hive game.

The Grasshopper class inherits from the abstract Piece class and encapsulates the unique characteristics and movement behavior of the Grasshopper piece in Hive. The Grasshopper can jump over a line of pieces to land on the opposite side.
