\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Represents a \doxylink{classhive_1_1models_1_1pieces_1_1Pillbug}{Pillbug} piece in the Hive game. 

The {\ttfamily \doxylink{classhive_1_1models_1_1pieces_1_1Pillbug}{Pillbug}} class inherits from the abstract {\ttfamily \doxylink{classhive_1_1models_1_1Piece}{Piece}} class and encapsulates the unique movement and special ability of the \doxylink{classhive_1_1models_1_1pieces_1_1Pillbug}{Pillbug} piece in Hive. The \doxylink{classhive_1_1models_1_1pieces_1_1Pillbug}{Pillbug} can move like a regular piece and also has the ability to pull adjacent pieces toward itself.
