\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Represents a \doxylink{classhive_1_1models_1_1pieces_1_1Grasshopper}{Grasshopper} piece in the Hive game. 

The {\ttfamily \doxylink{classhive_1_1models_1_1pieces_1_1Grasshopper}{Grasshopper}} class inherits from the abstract {\ttfamily \doxylink{classhive_1_1models_1_1Piece}{Piece}} class and encapsulates the unique characteristics and movement behavior of the \doxylink{classhive_1_1models_1_1pieces_1_1Grasshopper}{Grasshopper} piece in Hive. The \doxylink{classhive_1_1models_1_1pieces_1_1Grasshopper}{Grasshopper} can jump over a line of pieces to land on the opposite side.
